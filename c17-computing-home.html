Need help?

Use the right route
I am stuck on the work

Re-read the task, check the example or reference material, then ask your teacher with the exact point you are stuck on.

Something technical is broken

Record the error message, what you were trying to do and what you already tried. Avoid simply saying “it doesn't work”.

I need additional support

Speak to your teacher or the relevant college support team before a missed deadline becomes the problem.
